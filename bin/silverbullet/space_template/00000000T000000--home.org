Welcome. This space is a [[https://protesilaos.com/emacs/denote][Denote]]
library: every note carries its own metadata in its file name, so it stays
readable with or without this app, and Emacs can keep editing the same files.

The all-zero identifier is deliberate. It marks this page as the one the app
shipped rather than one you wrote, and it sorts before every real note. Rename
it, retitle it, or delete it — nothing depends on it existing.

* Getting around
- *⌘K* opens the page picker. Notes are listed by title, with their keywords
  beside them.
- Typing a title that does not exist offers to create it as a Denote note,
  which is =denote-open-or-create=.
- *⌘/* runs a command. Everything Denote-related is under =Denote:=.
- *TAB* on a headline cycles folding; *Shift-TAB* cycles the whole buffer.
- *Alt-j/k* move an item down/up, *Alt-l/h* indent and outdent it.
